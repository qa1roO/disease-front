A blind second opinion, generated 2026-10-29: Claude Haiku 5.5 saw only each cause's name and the 5-level scale (ADR 0001), never the proposal, and used no web search. Where it agrees with the proposal, the status is less likely to be a one-off judgement. Where it disagrees, look closer. This is a consistency check between two AI models, not a clinical review.

- **Agree: 138 of 173 causes** (79.2% of the assessed burden).
- **Disagree: 35**. **26** of them would move the cause across the front line (held 1–3 vs contested 4–5).
- **Pattern:** almost every disagreement is Treatable (proposal) vs Controlled (Haiku). Haiku counts "symptoms managed on ongoing treatment" as Controlled; the proposals keep Controlled for "near-normal lives on treatment" (ADR 0001) and use Treatable when the disease still progresses, relapses often, or most patients aren't well controlled. Deciding which reading the map uses settles most of this list at once.
- **Worth a look:** nasopharynx cancer. Haiku cites about 60–70% five-year survival, below the 80% bar the proposals use for Curable in cancers, so the proposal's Curable may be too generous.

- **Pattern:** acute infections that kill or harm many patients despite treatment.
