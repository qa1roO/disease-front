# Disease Map: v1 spec

Builds on [CONTEXT.md](../../CONTEXT.md) (vocabulary and decisions) and ADRs [0001](../adr/0001-five-level-status-scale.md), [0002](../adr/0002-icd11-is-the-map.md), [0003](../adr/0003-continuous-world-layout.md). Terms like *territory*, *province*, *status*, *provisional*, *fog*, *front line* and *burden* mean what CONTEXT.md says.

## Goal

A static website showing every ICD-11 disease (minus the agreed exclusions) as one continuous, zoomable strategy-game map. Provinces are sized by burden, coloured by status, and split by a front line between held and contested. v1 proves the whole chain end to end, from raw data to the map, on real data.

## In scope

1. The full ICD-11 map: chapters and blocks as territories, diseases as provinces, minus the exclusions.
2. Province sizes from GBD burden.
3. Provisional statuses for **every GBD cause on the map** (non-communicable, communicable, maternal, neonatal and nutritional), with sources, plus direct assessments of individual diseases that no GBD cause covers (smallpox first).
4. The **pipeline** progress bar.
5. The province info panel.
6. A static site you can deploy to GitHub Pages by hand.

## Out of scope for v1

- Assessing every individual disease. Direct assessments are added one by one, for diseases worth it; the ~1,000 diseases with no GBD cause stay in fog otherwise (they cover about 0.3% of the map).
- The activity and access bars (they show as "no data").
- The weekly scheduled job, status-change PRs, and automatic deploys.
- The "news from the front" feed and the timeline replay. The events log file exists, but nothing reads it yet.
- Other visual themes, mobile-specific layout, and search.

## What the user sees

### The map

- One continuous map on a dark "sea" background. Every province is drawn at once.
- **Pan** by dragging and **zoom** with the scroll wheel or pinch.
- Borders get heavier going up the hierarchy: thin between provinces, medium between blocks, heavy between chapters.
- **Labels change with zoom level**: chapter names when zoomed out, block names in the middle range, province names when zoomed in. A label appears only if it fits inside its region.
- **Province colour by status**: one colour for each of the 5 statuses, plus fog grey. A **provisional** status uses the same hue at a lighter tint (in v1, every assessed province is provisional).
- **Front line**: a bright, bold line between held and contested bands inside each block. The boundary between contested and fog is a thin dashed line.
- **Zoomed out**, each chapter is drawn as a summary: held | contested | fog bands by burden share, with one front line per chapter. It fades into the province detail as you zoom in (ADR 0003's fallback).
- **Legend**: the five statuses, provisional, fog, front line, and fog edge.

### Layout rules (from ADR 0003)

- Chapters → territories → provinces are nested weighted Voronoi cells inside one convex "continent". Below a chapter, territories nest to any depth (up to 7 levels in ICD-11 2026-01) and include both blocks and categories that have subtypes. Each cell's area is proportional to its burden.
- Within each parent, children with a higher held share are seeded further west.
- Each territory whose children are provinces is first cut into **held | contested | fog** bands across a fixed west → east direction, by burden share. Provinces are laid out inside their own band, held ones ordered by status (1 → 3) and contested ones by status (4 → 5).
- Every province gets a **minimum size**, so even the rarest stays clickable when zoomed in.
- The layout is deterministic: the same data always gives the same map.

### Info panel (click a province)

- Name, ICD-11 code, and position in the hierarchy (chapter › block › …).
- Status, with a "provisional" tag, the source link(s), and the nuance note (e.g. "curable if caught early").
- Pipeline bar: the furthest stage reached by any candidate therapy (No registered trial / Phase 1 / Phase 2 / Phase 3 / Approved), with its trial count and a link to the matching ClinicalTrials.gov search. ClinicalTrials.gov can't show preclinical work, so zero matching trials reads "No registered trial", not "Preclinical". For a held province, the bar shows "Approved".
- Activity and access bars, greyed out as "no data".
- Burden in DALYs, marked "size estimated" when split from a GBD cause.
- The GBD cause it belongs to.
- "Data as of" dates for each source.

Clicking a territory (chapter or block) shows its burden total and its held / contested / fog shares.

## Data pipeline

Python. Every stage reads files and writes files under `data/`, so each one can be re-run by itself. Stages 2, 5 and 6 are drafted from a Claude session: stage 2 because the IHME connector works only inside Claude, stages 5 and 6 by the owner's choice (2026-10-29), with no API key or API spend.

| # | Stage | Input | Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Fetch ICD-11** | WHO ICD-11 MMS 2026-01 simple tabulation and mapping tables (free download, no account) | `data/raw/icd11/` |
| 2 | **Fetch GBD** | GBD 2023 cause hierarchy, cause → ICD-10 code maps, and global DALYs per most-detailed cause (level 3 or 4) for 2023 (IHME, free account, non-commercial licence). A Claude-run step pulls them through the IHME GBD MCP connector (it works only inside Claude), or the files are downloaded by hand behind the IHME login. Drop the five overlapping aggregate causes (1026–1029, 1059). | `data/raw/gbd/` |
| 3 | **Build the tree** | stage 1 | `data/tree.json`: territories and provinces, with exclusions (non-disease chapters, injuries, "other specified" / "unspecified" entries) removed |
| 4 | **Cross-walk and burden** | stages 2–3 + WHO ICD-11 → ICD-10 mapping table (`11To10MapToOneCategory`) | `data/crosswalk.json` (province → GBD cause), `data/burden.json` (DALYs per province, plus the "estimated" flag) |
| 5 | **MeSH mapping** | every GBD cause, a Claude session's drafts in `data/raw/stage5-drafts/` (runbook: `pipeline/STAGE5.md`) | `data/mesh.yaml`: search terms per cause, `reviewed: false` until you review them; stage 7 uses reviewed ones only |
| 6 | **Status proposals** | GBD causes on the map and directly assessed diseases, a Claude session's drafts in `data/raw/stage6-drafts/` (runbook: `pipeline/STAGE6.md`), ChEMBL approved-drug indications (`drug_indication` with `max_phase_for_ind = 4`) | `data/proposals/*.yaml`, `data/assessments/*.yaml`, an `INDEX.md` in each |
| 7 | **Pipeline bar** | `mesh.yaml`, ClinicalTrials.gov API v2, ChEMBL approved-drug indications | `data/bars.json` |
| 8 | **Build site data** | everything above + approved statuses | `site/public/map.json` (one file, everything the front end needs) |

### Cross-walk rules (stage 4)

- Each ICD-11 disease → its one ICD-10 code (WHO `11To10MapToOneCategory` table) → the GBD cause that code belongs to.
- That code is an ICD-10 block range or chapter for 459 of 6,633 provinces (2026-01), and it may then span several GBD causes. In that case **majority rule** over the ICD-10 codes in the range applies. Ties are written to `data/crosswalk-review.yaml` for an LLM proposal and your decision.
- A category code is expanded to its ICD-10 leaf codes too, and each leaf votes (e.g. `E10` splits between type 1 diabetes and CKD due to type 1 diabetes, at `E10.2`).
- A leaf code's cause is looked up in this order, and the first list that holds it wins:
  1. GBD's cause-of-death lists;
  2. `data/gbd-extra-codes.yaml`, the project's own codes for causes GBD lists no codes for (depression, cataract, autism, GERD…);
  3. GBD's nonfatal lists. These are case definitions for hospital data and can be broad: bipolar disorder's runs `F30-F34.0`, which takes in every depressive episode. That's why the project's codes come first;
  4. a **GBD group**: the nearest coded level-2 or level-3 parent of causes that have no codes at all (liver cancer and cirrhosis by etiology, for example). It carries those causes' combined DALYs.
- A tie starts with the first candidate, in GBD order, as a placeholder. Edit it by hand in the review file; `null` leaves the province in fog.
- A disease with no GBD cause gets the **minimum size** (2% of the mean province burden) and is **fog**.
- A GBD cause's DALYs are **split equally** among its provinces. When there is more than one, each is marked "size estimated".
- The resulting table is the project's own work, labelled as such in `crosswalk.json` and on the site.

### Status proposals and review (stage 6)

For each GBD cause that has provinces on the map, and for each disease assessed directly (keyed by its ICD-11 code), a Claude session researches the status with web search and writes a draft (status, nuance, sources, MeSH headings) into git-ignored `data/raw/stage6-drafts/`. `python -m pipeline 6` checks the drafts and writes the proposals:

```yaml
gbd_cause: "Ischemic heart disease"
gbd_cause_id: 493
status: 3            # 1–5
nuance: "..."        # optional, typical-patient caveat
sources:
  - url: https://...
    title: "..."
mesh:                # MeSH descriptors for the cross-check, resolved from the draft's headings
  D003324: Coronary Artery Disease
cross_check: ok      # or "flag: claims Curable but ChEMBL has no approved drug"
approved: false      # you flip this to true after reviewing
fingerprint: ...     # lets re-runs tell an edited file from an untouched one
```

- Every source URL must resolve (HTTP 200) and every MeSH heading must exist, or the draft is rejected with the reason, to be fixed in the session and re-run.
- Re-runs keep every existing proposal; `--refresh` rewrites only those that are neither approved nor edited.
- The **ChEMBL cross-check** flags any status that disagrees with the approved-drug data, for example "Curable" with no approved drug, or "Untreatable" with one.
- Only `approved: true` proposals reach the site, when stage 8 runs. Each approval that changes a status appends an entry to `data/events.json`, which records the date, cause, old status and new status.

### Pipeline bar (stage 7)

- Search ClinicalTrials.gov for each GBD cause's reviewed MeSH terms, interventional studies only. Each term is an exact `ConditionMeshTerm` match, ORed together. ClinicalTrials.gov indexes conditions under MeSH 2025 labels, so `mesh.yaml` keeps those where 2026 renamed a heading.
- The bar value is the **furthest phase** among trials that are recruiting, enrolling by invitation, active or completed. Count only trials first posted in the 10 years before the API's data date, so long-abandoned programmes don't count. Early Phase 1 counts as Phase 1. Phase 4 (post-approval) trials count as Phase 3. Phase "NA" trials (devices, behavioural) count toward the trial total but don't set a phase.
- `data/bars.json` holds, per cause, the phase, the trial count and the matching ClinicalTrials.gov search URL (all phases; the site's phase filter shows the breakdown).
- The bar is **Approved** when ChEMBL has an indication with `max_phase_for_ind = 4` for one of the cause's reviewed MeSH terms.
- Throttle to about 1 request per second and back off on HTTP 429. No official rate limit is published. Record the API's `dataTimestamp` as the "data as of" date. Counts are cached per `dataTimestamp`, so a re-run resumes rather than repeating requests.
- Provinces inherit their GBD cause's bar, like their status. A held province (status 1–3) shows Approved, since a treatment that cures or controls it exists.

## Front end

- Vite + TypeScript + D3 (with `d3-weighted-voronoi` and `d3-voronoi-map`), in `site/`.
- Loads `map.json` once and computes the layout in the browser. If computing the layout takes more than ~2 s on real data, move it into stage 8 as a Node build step and ship precomputed polygons instead.
- Rendering: SVG to start. Switch to Canvas only if panning stutters on real data.
- `npm run build` produces a static `site/dist/`, which you publish to GitHub Pages by hand in v1.
- The site shows the attributions each source requires (see [docs/research/data-sources.md](../research/data-sources.md)):
  - the ICD-11 citation with its CC BY-ND 3.0 IGO notice;
  - "Source: Institute for Health Metrics and Evaluation. Used with permission. All rights reserved.";
  - ClinicalTrials.gov, with its processing date;
  - ChEMBL, with its release number.

  Every province and territory keeps its ICD-11 code, title and URI in `map.json`.

## Acceptance criteria

1. `python -m pipeline all` runs stages 1–8 (or skips any stage whose output is up to date) and produces `site/public/map.json`.
2. The map shows all ICD-11 disease chapters except the excluded ones. Their province count matches `tree.json`.
3. Every province's size traces to a GBD cause or is the minimum size, and the info panel says which.
4. Every GBD cause on the map has an approved status with at least one working source link. Provinces with neither a GBD cause nor a direct assessment are fog.
5. Every block with both held and contested provinces shows exactly one straight front line.
6. Clicking any province opens the info panel with all the fields above, and missing data reads "no data", never blank or broken.
7. The map loads and becomes interactive in under 3 s on a mid-range laptop, and panning is smooth.
8. The same data produces the same map on every load.

## Risks and things to verify first

- **Data access**: verified in [docs/research/data-sources.md](../research/data-sources.md). GBD data comes through the owner's IHME account, via the MCP connector or manual downloads. Raw GBD files may not be redistributed. Derived per-province DALYs may be committed.
- **Licences**: decided 2026-10-29, with no permission requested from WHO or IHME (non-commercial pet project).
  - The ICD-11 crosswalk clause (§1.2.4) is an accepted risk. `crosswalk.json` and the site label the mapping as the project's own, not WHO's.
  - ChEMBL is CC BY-SA 3.0, so committed files derived from it must be share-alike. Add a `data/LICENSE` note before the repo goes public.
- **Real province count**: resolved. 6,633 provinces after exclusions (ICD-11 2026-01), so layout and rendering stay in the browser.
- **Mixed territories**: 532 ICD-11 parents have both provinces and sub-territories as children. The held | contested | fog banding is defined for territories whose children are all provinces. How to band a mixed parent (e.g. treat its direct provinces as one extra child territory) is undecided.
- **Cross-walk coverage**: how many ICD-11 diseases end up with no GBD cause, and therefore in fog. If it's a large share, the map looks emptier than intended.
- **Front-line readability when zoomed out**: resolved 2026-10-29. On real data (all 173 proposals previewed as approved), only 68 territories hold both held and contested provinces, and zoomed out their front lines read as scattered ticks. The map now uses ADR 0003's fallback: zoomed out, each chapter is one held | contested | fog summary with one front line, fading into the province detail between 1.5× and 3× the fitted zoom.
- **Status quality**: the line between Controlled and Treatable is a judgement call. Expect to spend real review time on stage 6.
